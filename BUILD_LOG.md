# Build Log

Dated record of decisions, dead ends and reasons. Newest entries at the bottom of each day.

## Sunday 4 October 2026 (Phase 0: data audit and problem lock)

- Started the build in an empty folder. Initialised git and a dedicated virtual environment (`.venv`, Python 3.12.7). Chose 3.12 over the also-installed 3.14 for wheel availability and Streamlit Community Cloud compatibility.
- Read the brief. Its "Saturday, October 11" is a Sunday in 2026, so I planned to finish by Saturday 10 October to be safe.
- Confirmed internet access. NBS, UBEC, HDX and UNICEF downloads work. `grid3.gov.ng` timed out; its education layer is mirrored on HDX so that was not a blocker. `open.africa` returned 403.
- **Dead end:** GRID3 school points carry teacher and student fields, but only ~7.5% are filled, none in 15 states, and some are corrupt (teacher total 643 million). Not usable for ratios.
- **Dead end:** Niger State is largely missing from GRID3 (16 of 25 LGAs with zero schools). A naive access index would have flagged Niger for missing data rather than need. This is why every ranking needs a data-confidence grade.
- **Dead end:** NBS WAEC 2019-2021 is image-only and broken down by geopolitical zone, not state. State WAEC exists only for 2016-2018.
- UBEC digest 2019/2020 is state level only. Its own method note says 2019-2020 values are extrapolated from the 2018 personnel audit plus a 4.5% school sample.
- **Breakthrough:** the Federal Ministry of Education's DNEMIS portal publishes the 2024 Annual School Census as public parquet files: 774 LGAs with public-school learners, teachers, usable classrooms, toilets, and expected vs actual census returns. This gives measured LGA teacher data, fresher than every other source.
- DNEMIS quality: 101 LGAs returned under half their expected census forms; Rivers, Cross River and Nasarawa are near-blind. Decision: rank only LGAs passing completeness and plausibility checks, and list the rest as "insufficient data" with the reason, rather than imputing.
- Wrote `scripts/fetch_data.py` so all raw data can be re-downloaded, with a provenance log (URL, date, size, SHA-256).
- Proposed scope: see `docs/DATA_AUDIT.md`.

## Tuesday 6 October 2026 (scope locked)

- **Scope locked:** proceed as proposed. Use DNEMIS data with attribution and a note that no explicit licence is stated.
- Evaluated three further Kaggle datasets. All downloaded without login.
  - NMIS education facilities (2009-2014): recognised as a mirror of the official NMIS release (CC BY 4.0, energydata.info). The official host blocks scripted downloads, so we keep the Kaggle copy and cite the official source. A quick check gives Spearman 0.57 between 2014 and 2024 LGA learner-teacher ratios. **Kept** as a persistent-shortage signal and 2014 facility-condition context.
  - MICS6 2021 state workbook: a community transcription of the same UNICEF tables we already hold. **Kept as a cross-check** for our own extraction, not as a primary source.
  - JAMB 2024 student performance: synthetic by the author's own description, no geography. **Rejected and deleted.**
- Added both kept Kaggle sources to `scripts/fetch_data.py` and the provenance log. Pinned scipy.
- Built the LGA name crosswalk (`pipeline/crosswalk.py`): exact normalised match within state, then a hand-checked alias table (`config/lga_aliases.csv`), then a strict fuzzy match (cutoff 0.85, must be a clear winner), with any two rows landing on one P-code both rejected. DNEMIS: 774/774 matched (743 exact, 22 fuzzy, 9 alias). NMIS: 769/772; the 3 left over are ambiguous and documented in `config/unmatched_reasons.csv`. Every fuzzy match was reviewed by eye. "Munya" to Muya is correct: Niger has 25 LGAs and Munya is a common spelling of Muya. Yewa North and South are the post-2002 names of Egbado North and South.
- **Design change:** enrolment divided by projected school-age population tracks reporting completeness (median 0.09 in LGAs under 50% reporting vs 0.70 above 90%). So "hidden demand" cannot be an index component; it is now a flag shown only where reporting is at least 90%.
- **Design change:** a first rule excluded LGAs whose learners exceeded 1.6x the projected population. That removed 45 LGAs, mostly in Kano, Katsina, Yobe and Oyo, where the 2006-based projection and the inclusion of integrated Qur'anic and senior secondary learners explain the excess. Excluding them would have quietly pushed northern LGAs out. New rule: above 2.5x is not ranked (Dutsi, Potiskum, Ibi, Tofa, Takum); 1.6x to 2.5x is ranked with a "verify learner count" caveat that costs one confidence grade.
- Index: weighted mean of percentile ranks (teacher 0.50, classroom 0.35, toilet 0.15) among ranked LGAs. Result: 642 ranked (A 398, B 156, C 88), 132 not ranked with reasons. Spearman with the first principal component 0.987. Under 1,000 Dirichlet weight draws, 15 of the top 20 stay in the top 20 at least 80% of the time.
- "Main driver" first used the largest weighted contribution, which made every LGA say "Teacher shortage". Changed to the dimension where the LGA is most extreme relative to others, restricted to teachers or classrooms (toilets stay in the score but are not what this planner deploys).
- Added `python -m pipeline.build` and 16 pytest tests (crosswalk, ranking invariants, gap arithmetic, reproducible perturbation, "teachers to exit top 20" actually exits, partner CSV adapter).
- **WAEC 2016-2018 extraction** (`pipeline/extract_waec.py`) reads word coordinates, not the scrambled text stream. School ("public") candidates parse for all 37 states x 3 years. Checks: male + female = total, recomputed percentages, 37 units, and states + Offshore against the report's own national page. 2016 and 2017 reconcile exactly. 2018 is short by 17,506 candidates and 9,434 passes: Taraba 2018 is printed as 75 candidates (21,754 in 2017), so the national page implies about 17,581. **Taraba 2018 excluded from modelling, not imputed.** Also logged: the executive summary misprints the 2018 private total (109,798 vs 109,978 on the national page); Borno school candidates doubled 2016 to 2017 (plausible relocation, kept and flagged). Private-candidate panels with layout failures are logged; private candidates are not used.
- **UBEC 2018-2020 extraction** (`pipeline/extract_ubec.py`): 10 trend tables, 37 states each, parts sum to totals and states sum to the TOTAL rows. One published inconsistency (Abia 2020 primary enrolment), in a year we do not use.
- **MICS6 2021 validation data** (`pipeline/extract_mics.py`): primary out-of-school rate extracted from the UNICEF fact sheet and matched the Kaggle workbook for all 37 states. The foundational skills table has no text layer, so I transcribed all 37 reading and numeracy totals by eye from the rendered page (`config/mics_p23_visual_check.csv`); the workbook matches on every value, so it is used for those two columns.
- **Lens 2 negative result.** Ridge on six 2018 UBEC resource measures, leave-one-state-out: RMSE 17.3 points vs 17.2 for the national mean (out-of-sample R2 = -0.02). The 90% interval is +/-32 points, so expected-vs-actual flags would be meaningless. I did not search predictor combinations (37 states, overfitting risk). A pre-planned check shows WAEC pass rates do track independent learning measures (MICS reading rho +0.75, numeracy +0.74, out-of-school -0.82), while resource measures relate weakly (|rho| <= 0.37). **Decision:** Lens 2 becomes "state outcome context" (WAEC alongside MICS) and the negative test is reported openly; no state is labelled an over- or under-performer. This also supports Lens 1's design: rank measured gaps against a standard, not predicted performance.
- **Lens 1 external validation** (`pipeline/validate.py`): the learner-weighted state need score correlates with MICS out-of-school +0.46, reading -0.49, numeracy -0.59 (35 states, all p < 0.01). DNEMIS 2024 vs UBEC 2018 state learner-teacher ratio +0.61. None of these were inputs.
- `python -m pipeline.build` now runs every extraction, both lenses and validation. 24 tests pass (8 new reconciliation tests that encode the known misprints, so any new discrepancy fails the suite).
- **App** (`streamlit_app.py`): three tabs (where to send teachers; state outcome context; method, data and limits). Opens on the national top 20 with a state filter, grade filter, CSV download, map, LGA detail panel and a "Not ranked" list with reasons. Charts follow the dataviz reference palette: one blue sequential ramp for need, neutral grey for not ranked, single-series scatter, grades shown as text not colour. Smoke-tested with Streamlit AppTest (national view, Kano, Rivers with no ranked LGAs, empty grade filter): no exceptions.
- **Dead end:** the first map render filled the whole frame blue. Cause: RFC 7946 GeoJSON uses counter-clockwise outer rings and Plotly geo expects clockwise. Fixed in the build by reorienting rings (`pipeline/build.py::_clockwise`). Found by rendering to PNG (`scripts/render_figures.py`), which lets the map be checked without a browser.
- Added `scripts/reproduce.py` (fetch, build, test, render in one command), Streamlit theme, rewritten README with screenshots.
- **Deliverables drafted:** one-page write-up (`docs/WRITEUP.md`, rendered by `scripts/render_writeup.py`, which injects the top-10 table from the processed data and fails unless the PDF is exactly one page). Demo mode (`?demo=1`) shows a numbered on-screen path that the demo video follows step by step.

## Saturday 10 October 2026 (insights beyond the ranking)

- Reviewed the 85 DNEMIS indicators; the ranking used six. Added `pipeline/insights.py`, shared by the build and the app, for five questions a planner asks next: move or hire, women teachers, junior secondary seats, private-school alternative, change since 2014. Plus ten context measures in the LGA detail view.
- **Data checks before use:** female teachers exist for public schools specifically (`PbzDc38hOsx` in `fact_ownership`), so used that rather than the all-schools series. Only 34% of expected private-school forms were returned nationally, so private share is shown only where at least half came back (284 ranked LGAs). JSS ratios only where at least 75% of JSS forms came back (414). Dropout, orphans, computers and non-teaching staff are published only for all schools together and are labelled "all schools". **Dead end:** boarding learners sum to 838 million nationally, an obvious entry error; not used.
- **Move or hire:** spare teachers (above 35:1) move within the state to the neediest ranked LGAs first, because SUBEBs employ teachers by state. Even with every spare teacher moving, moves cover 21,487 of the 380,212 shortfall (6%). 20 states where moves fall short hold 98% of the shortfall.
- **Bug found and fixed:** the first allocation loop stopped at the first LGA with no teacher shortfall. A high-need LGA can be short of classrooms but not teachers, so moves were undercounted (5.4% before the fix). Added a test for it. Also corrected a summary that counted states with no shortfall as states where "moves close the gap".
- **Values no dataset holds are inputs, not assumptions:** share of spare teachers who can move (default 100%, labelled an upper bound), JSS target transition (default 100%, the UBE nine-year policy), flag thresholds (in `config/settings.yaml`), and unit costs and budget (no default at all). The budget planner funds LGAs in priority order and shows nothing until costs are entered.
- App: new tabs "Move or hire?", "Budget planner" and "Beyond the ratio"; LGA detail and top list extended; demo path updated. 35 tests pass. Write-up gains one paragraph and still renders to one page.
